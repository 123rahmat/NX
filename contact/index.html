FAQ

Common questions

How do I get a quotation?

Send the request form on this page or message us on WhatsApp. We review your requirements and reply with a written scope, timeline and itemised price.

Which technologies do you work with?

For devices: ESP32, STM32 (ARM Cortex-M), Arduino and Raspberry Pi. For software: web platforms, Android and iOS apps, cloud APIs and MQTT. If you already have systems in place, we build on them.

Do I need hardware before we start?

No. Many projects are software only. When a device is needed, we simulate it first and recommend suitable boards and sensors before anything is purchased.

Can I visit your office?

Yes. Our office is in Islamabad. Please call or message us first so we can arrange a time.

What happens after delivery?

You receive the working system, documentation and a short user guide. Ongoing maintenance and support can be agreed as part of your quotation.
