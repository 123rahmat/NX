Mobile apps and dashboards

We build screens that show what your system is doing, send commands and raise alerts, designed for the people who use them every day.

Apps and dashboards are designed together with the firmware and connection, so the whole system works as one.

What we build

Android and iOS apps

Control devices, view live data and receive alerts on a phone.

Web dashboards

Charts, history and reports in the browser for operators and managers.

Device setup apps

Pair and configure devices over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi from a phone.

User and access management

Enrol and remove users and control who can see and do what.
