One team for the whole connected system

Nexwire is an engineering and software company based in Islamabad. We build the systems organisations depend on, from firmware on a device to the web platform and application their teams use.

Our story

Devices, connectivity and software that work as one

Most organisations do not need an isolated tool. They need devices, connectivity, platforms and applications that work together as one system.

We take responsibility for that whole system. We define the requirement, model it where hardware is involved, build and test the software, and deliver a platform your team can operate.

Every engagement starts with a written scope and quotation, and ends with a documented, working system and a support arrangement.

Protocols

We speak your systems' language

MQTT

Lightweight publish and subscribe for sensors and commands.

HTTP / HTTPS

REST APIs and web services between devices and servers.

TCP

Reliable data streams, for example device to PC or server.

UDP

Fast, low-latency telemetry where an occasional loss is acceptable.

WebSocket

Live two-way updates for dashboards.

Modbus

Industrial meters, drives and controllers.

CoAP

Constrained devices on low-power networks.

TLS

Encryption for data in transit.
