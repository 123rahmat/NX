One team, accountable for the whole system

Linqore is an engineering and software company based in Islamabad. We build websites, apps and connected devices, and when they need to work together, we take ownership of the whole system.

Our story

Devices, connectivity and software that work as one

Most organisations do not need another isolated tool. They need devices, connectivity, platforms and applications that work together reliably as one system.

That is the responsibility we take on, whether the job is a single website or a full device-to-dashboard system. We define the requirement, prototype and test before committing your budget, and hand over something your team can run with confidence.

Every engagement begins with a written scope and quotation, and ends with a documented, working system and an agreed support arrangement.

Test before we build

We prototype, simulate and test early, so problems are caught while they are still cheap to fix, on screens and on circuit boards alike.

One team, one system

Firmware, connectivity and applications are designed together by one team, so every part fits and nothing falls between suppliers.

Clear scope, clear price

You know exactly what will be delivered, when, and at what cost before any work begins.

Connectivity

Communication links and protocols

We connect devices over the link that suits the site, and use the protocol your systems already speak.

Wi-Fi

Local networks and direct cloud access for indoor and campus systems.

  • ESP32
  • ESP8266
  • Provisioning

GSM and LTE

Remote sites with no Wi-Fi, using mobile data or SMS alerts.

  • SIM800L
  • GPRS data
  • SMS

Bluetooth

Short-range pairing and setup from a phone app.

  • BLE
  • Classic
  • App setup

Serial and wired

Boards, sensors and industrial equipment on a wire.

  • UART
  • SPI
  • I2C
  • RS-485
  • CAN
Protocols

Industry-standard protocols, securely implemented

MQTT

Lightweight publish and subscribe for sensors and commands.

HTTP / HTTPS

REST APIs and web services between devices and servers.

TCP

Reliable data streams, for example device to PC or server.

UDP

Fast, low-latency telemetry where an occasional loss is acceptable.

WebSocket

Live two-way updates for dashboards.

Modbus

Industrial meters, drives and controllers.

CoAP

Constrained devices on low-power networks.

TLS

Encryption for data in transit.
