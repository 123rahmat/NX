Privacy policy

How this website works and how we handle your information.

Last updated 6 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Linqore ("we", "us"), a digital solutions company in Islamabad. We decide why and how the personal data described here is used. You can reach us at the email address or phone number on our Contact page.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, a phone number or email, your city, the service you are interested in, an optional budget, and your message. We do not ask for anything else, and we do not ask for sensitive data. Please do not put passwords, payment details or health information in the form.

What we do not collect

This website has no accounts and no advertising, and it does not use analytics, tracking pixels or cookies. We do not profile visitors or make automated decisions about them.

Why we use it and our legal basis

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and, if you go ahead, deliver the work. We handle your data in line with the laws of Pakistan, including the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016. We rely on taking steps at your request before a contract, and on our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Who receives your data

The contact form is delivered by FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, which passes your message to our inbox (an email provider acts for us there). These providers may process data in other countries. We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as it takes to deal with it, and afterwards only as long as we need to keep a record of our work together or meet legal duties. Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California and other US states may also ask what we hold, and we do not sell or share personal information. We will not treat you differently for asking. Contact us to use any of these rights; we will reply within one month. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, runs no accounts or database of its own, and restricts what scripts can run in your browser. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is for businesses and adults. It is not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has sent us data, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above. For any privacy question or request, use the email address or phone number on our Contact page.
