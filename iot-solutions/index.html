Questions

Common questions

What if the site has no Wi-Fi?

We use GSM or LTE mobile data, or SMS alerts, for remote sites without Wi-Fi.

Is the data secure?

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS, and devices authenticate to the server.

Can you connect to systems we already use?

Yes. We integrate with existing servers, industrial equipment over Modbus and RS-485, and third-party APIs.
