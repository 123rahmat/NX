IoT solutions: devices, connectivity and cloud

We connect devices securely to a server: data storage, remote commands, alerts and over-the-air updates, all designed as one system.

We choose the link that suits the site, whether Wi-Fi indoors, GSM at remote sites or Bluetooth for setup from a phone, and use the protocol your systems already speak.

What we build

Remote monitoring

Live readings from sensors, meters and machines, viewable from anywhere.

Remote control

Switch relays, change settings and send commands from a dashboard or phone.

Alerts

SMS, app or email alerts when a value crosses a limit or a device goes offline.

Over-the-air updates

Update device firmware remotely without visiting the site.

Questions

What if the site has no Wi-Fi?

We use GSM or LTE mobile data, or SMS alerts, for remote sites without Wi-Fi.

Is the data secure?

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS, and devices authenticate to the server.

Can you connect to systems we already use?

Yes. We integrate with existing servers, industrial equipment over Modbus and RS-485, and third-party APIs.
